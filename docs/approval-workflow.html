How a request is approved

The path every Purchase Requisition, Request for Payment and Reimbursement takes, from the requester's draft to completion. Each band is one person or team; the bottom band is what the portal does by itself.

Requester→ Department Head→ OPS processing→ President→ Completed

Rules along the way

  • Signature required. Submitting and approving both apply the person's own e-signature, which is printed on the form.
  • Any approver can approve, return for revision, reject, or ask the requester a question. A question keeps the request waiting until it is answered.
  • A returned request starts again from the department head when it is resubmitted; earlier decisions stay on record.
  • The requester can cancel while a request is pending approval or returned.
  • Department heads' own requests skip the department head step and go straight on.
  • Prepared for someone else: a colleague can fill in the request, but only the requester can submit it, because it carries their signature.
  • Out of office: an approver can name a colleague to approve in their place; the history shows who acted for whom.
  • The President's approval is final. If a Finance step still appears in a route, remove it under Administration → Approval Workflows.
  • Route changes apply only to requests submitted afterwards; requests already in progress keep their route.
Statuses and step names match the portal. Steps can be changed by an administrator under Administration → Approval Workflows, so check there for the routes currently in use.
